Our Vision

Wirtuu Qaroomina Islaamaa

Dhaloota Qur'aanaa beekumsa, amantii, akhlaaqa fi dandeettii ammayyaa qabu ijaaruu. Kunis mul'ata keenya isa guddaa dha.

I — BEEKUMSA

Iddoo Beekumsi Hikmaa Maddisiisu

Qur'aana fi Ilmii Shari'aa bu'uuraan barachuu, hubachuu fi beekumsa sana jireenya keessatti hojiiirra oolchuuf qophii.

العِلْمُ نُورٌ
II — HIKMAA

Iddoo Hikmaan Ogummaa Uumu

Beekumsa qofti gahaa miti; hikmaan beekumsa karaa sirriin fayyadamuuf nu gargaara. Tarbiyaan beekumsa hikmaatti jijjiiruuf hojjenna.

الحِكْمَةُ ضَالَّةُ الْمُؤْمِنِ
III — OGUMMAA

Iddoo Dhaloonni Jaarraa 21ffaa Itti Ijaaramu

Qur'aana, beekumsa Shari'aa, teknooloojii fi dandeettii ammayyaa walitti fiduun dhaloota jireenya jijjiiruuf qophaa'e ijaaruu.

وَعَلَّمَ آدَمَ الْأَسْمَاءَ كُلَّهَا
